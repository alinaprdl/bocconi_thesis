Cristina Scocchia, CEO of Kiko Milano and board director for EssilorLuxottica and Illy, discusses her experiences meeting the challenge of COVID-19. In an interview with Sally Elliott, co-leader of Spencer Stuart’s Retail, Apparel & Luxury Goods Practice, Scocchia also shares insights on how she and her team are moving from early survival mode to planning and investing for the future.

Read the complete publication, The Retail World 2020 - Retailing in a Time of Crisis: Issue 7, originally distributed by World Retail Congress.
