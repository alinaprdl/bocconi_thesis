Career Opportunity: Regional Managing Director Americas, CFA Institute

About the CFA Institute

CFA Institute is the global association of investment professionals that sets the standard for professional excellence and credentials. The organization is a champion of ethical behavior in investment markets and a respected source of knowledge in the global financial community. Our aim is to create an environment where investors’ interests come first, markets function at their best, and economies grow. There are more than 178,000 CFA charterholders worldwide in 162 markets. CFA Institute has nine offices worldwide and there are 159 local member societies. For more information, visit www.cfainstitute.org or follow us on Twitter at @CFAInstitute and on Facebook.com/CFAInstitute.

Mission and Vision

Mission Statement

The mission of CFA Institute is to lead the investment profession globally by promoting the highest standards of ethics, education, and professional excellence for the ultimate benefit of society

Mission

The mission of CFA Institute is served by generating value for core investment management professionals and engaging with the core investment management industry to advance ethics, market integrity, and professional standards of practice, which collectively contributes value to society.

Vision

The vision of CFA Institute is to serve all finance professionals seeking investment management-related education, knowledge, professional development, connection, or inspiration. They also aim to lead the investment profession's thinking in the areas of ethics, capital market integrity, and excellence of practice.

CFA Institute seeks to set professional standards for investment management practitioners and broadly engage other finance professionals through their interest and interactions with the investment management industry. Improving outcomes for investors advances our social mission and benefits members through greater demand for educated and ethical investment management professionals.

Principles

CFA Institute believes that financial markets and services should operate in the following ways:

Investment professionals contribute to the ultimate benefit of society through the sustainable value generated by efficient financial markets and by effective investment institutions.

Good stewardship and high ethical standards are necessary for trust and confidence to be secured and for society to be served.

Financial markets should afford every investor the opportunity to earn a fair return. Financial markets are more effective with knowledgeable, diverse participants.

High ethical principles and professional standards are essential to positive outcomes; rules and regulations, while necessary, are not sufficient by themselves.

In addition to direct benefits to their clients, the work of investment management professionals and the impact of the investment management industry also benefits society through improved investor outcomes and the resulting effective allocation of capital that drives economic growth and development.

As a global, independent organization, CFA Institute plays a role in being a steward and champion of the investment management industry.

Cultural Values

The organization’s Cultural Values define who they are, what they stand for, and what they aspire to be. They run deeply through the organization, guiding their internal and external behaviors. They bind colleagues together, across every level of business, in passionate pursuit of the organization’s mission.

Four Strategic Pillars

The organization’s mission continues to evolve with an opportunity for more ambitious programs and additional growth prospects. CFA Institute has organized this growth around four strategic pillars and Customer Centric Approach is a key component of this strategy.

The Role

Background on the role

Reporting to Lutfey Siddiqi, Managing Director, Regions and Society Relations, the Regional MD, Americas will further advance the mission of CFA Institute through the guidance, coordination and support of organizational resources and staff in the region. S/he will be responsible for delivering continuous value to CFA Institute stakeholders and constituents by building and maintaining robust meaningful relationships with Societies, institutions and universities that strengthen the local relevance and influence.

The emphasis is on influencing and building relationships, advocating for the highest standards of practice and ethics among regulators and members, and promoting financial market integrity and transparency. The Regional MD will be an industry leader, with the requisite executive presence and communications skills.

Working with service line heads, the leader will oversee the distribution of CFA Institute’s products in the region, raising awareness of the organization’s offerings while at the same time, feed back into the product design process on behalf of members and clients in the region.

Key Accountabilities

Defining and executing a short- and long-term regional business plan that supports the Corporate strategy, and aligns with the Corporate mission

Ensuring the corporate culture and values (Trust, Inclusion, Curiosity and Service) are consistently modeled in all business communications, actions and related activities

Leading growth of members, candidates and other clients in multiple distribution channels: Societies, Institutions and Universities

Managing and monitoring regional activities, and setting and executing regional goals

Developing and nurturing effective collaboration with other Regional leaders for a consistent global approach

Collaborating with strategic product and function leaders to ensure the successful delivery of goals

Identify opportunities to increase awareness of CFA Institute programs in regional/ local target markets

Build a network of external relationships in order to raise awareness of CFA Institute and its offerings, to garner support for its views, and to provide a voice on behalf of its members

Promote awareness of the organization, including its products, brand, messages, and identity to all audiences in the region

Build industry relationships, extend employer outreach, and enhance networks with educational institutions, including participation in high profile industry and educational events in major markets

Represent CFA Institute at conferences and other industry events.

Additional Shared Responsibilities

Along with Advocacy, Research & Standards:

Provide with input on key topics of interest for the Region

Align around advocacy, strategy, and planning for the Region

Propose adjustments to Standards based on feedback from stakeholders

Support advocacy, standards and similar efforts with Societies

Along with Credentialing & Professional Learning:

Provide with voice of the customer from the Region

Align around distribution strategy for the different products and provide with updates on the performance

Along with Marketing:

Elaborate marketing plan for the Region following global guidelines

Collaborate with marketing to help enhance the customer experience

Along with Finance:

Set performance targets for the Region

Follow performance against financial targets, as well as other relevant KPIs

Reports To

Lutfey Siddiqi, CFA Managing Director, Regions and Society Relations

Lutfey Siddiqi leads the organization’s engagement, liaison, and partnership with members and stakeholders, including industry and academia. His regional and society relations teams around the world are charged with relationship management, representing the voice of the customer, and delivering on the CFA Institute mission in alignment with local market contexts.

Lutfey is a Visiting Professor-in-Practice at the London School of Economics (IDEAS) and an Adjunct Professor at the National University of Singapore (Risk Management Institute). He is a member of the World Economic Forum Future Council on Fiscal & Monetary Policy, having previously served on the councils for the future of financing and long-term investing. His industry experience includes stints at Deutsche Bank, Barclays and latterly UBS Investment Bank where he was Global Head of Emerging Markets for FX, Rates and Credit, and founding head of UBS Knowledge Network.

Lutfey was born in Bangladesh, went to school in the UAE, Namibia and Wales, university in England, and has worked in London, Zurich, and Singapore, mostly in multi-regional, client-facing roles.
