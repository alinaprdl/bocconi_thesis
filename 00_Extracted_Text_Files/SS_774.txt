Former CEO of FESTO AG (until 2016) and CEO of 4.0-Veit GbR

| 1 min read

Dr. Eberhard Veit talks to Dr. Oliver Köster, Spencer Stuart, about the challenges and opportunities facing industrial and manufacturing businesses seeking to integrate new automation and data exchange technologies. Dr. Veit has been tasked by the German government to lead the advisory board overseeing the development of the Industry 4.0 platform.
