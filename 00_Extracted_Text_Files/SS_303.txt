20th Annual CMO Summit

Highlights from our 20th CMO Summit, where a group of the world’s preeminent marketing experts looked ahead to the future of the marketing function.

To celebrate its milestone 20th annual CMO Summit — and its first in-person gathering in three years — Spencer Stuart hosted an intimate gathering of top marketing leaders for a rich, interactive discussion. The illuminating conversation focused on two key questions: What had been the most important influence on the marketing function in general over the past 20 years, and what changes in marketing leadership do you expect in the next 5-10 years?
