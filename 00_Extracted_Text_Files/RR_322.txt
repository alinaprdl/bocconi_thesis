Lessons on Success from Women on Middle Eastern Boards

How Did They Make It?

There has been a growing number of women on boards in the Middle East over the last 20 years. So, what can we learn from these trailblazers? We interviewed five inspiring female leaders across the region to find out.

Every one of them asserted the importance of continuous learning and an openness to change. None had a smooth journey to the top, so relied on their ability to adapt to challenging circumstances.

Click the pictures below to read the full interviews with these inspiring women and learn:

What lesson has had the biggest impact on their career

What enabled them to get to the top

What advice they have for the next generation of women leaders.
