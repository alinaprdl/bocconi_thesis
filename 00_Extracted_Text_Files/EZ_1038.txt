Inclusive LeadershipBBC.com – Helping Women Clear Career Break Hurdles and Return to Work

March 2017

1 min read

Share

Share on LinkedIn

Share via Email

Vodafone recently announced plans to actively recruit women on career breaks, many of whom have left their jobs to have children, reports BBC.com. According to Egon Zehnder’s Fiona Packman, women who return to work after having a child face a number of barriers, including pay penalties. "They face a lot of challenges. Not all organisations, both public and private sector, have been very adept at how they help those women overcome those challenges," notes Packman.

Full story: Rob Young: Vodafone to recruit women on career breaks on BBC.com (3 March 2017).
