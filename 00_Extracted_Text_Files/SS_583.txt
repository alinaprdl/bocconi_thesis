Lessons learned from the mining supercycle

November 2018

| 1 min read

Matthew Chamberlain, CEO of London Metal Exchange, discusses Mining for Value, a new book from Spencer Stuart and CESCO, at its launch at LME Week 2018 in London.

Mining for Value examines what went wrong during the supercycle and offers key insights from senior figures who experienced the cycle firsthand, and supports the next generation of leaders as they seek to build a more robust, diverse and sustainable industry for the future.
