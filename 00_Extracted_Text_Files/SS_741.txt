Highlights from the Global Board of Directors Survey

December 2016

| 1 min read

As part of one of the most comprehensive surveys of corporate directors around the world (4,000-plus directors from 60 countries), Spencer Stuart; Professor Boris Groysberg and Yo-Jud Cheng from Harvard Business School, and WomenCorporateDirectors Foundation, led by Susan Stautberg; and independent researcher Deborah Bell; explore how directors feel about the economy, board effectiveness and compensation — and uncover where male and female directors diff­er on these issues here.
