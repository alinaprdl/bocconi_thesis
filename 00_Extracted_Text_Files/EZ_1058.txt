Hub Magazine – Eric Anderson: The Trademarks of Top Technology Leaders

What kind of technology CEOs can really foster innovation, asks Egon Zehnder’s Eric Anderson in Hub Magazine? Successful technology leaders are usually comfortable with ambiguity, complexity and shifting risks. On top of outstanding influencing and communication skills, they also need to be exemplary mentors, he adds. For Anderson: “CEOs […] need to promote professional development that connects with an individual’s values and priorities.” In the face of constant disruption, top technology leaders also act as a strong compass for their companies, offering clear guidance at all times.

Full story: Eric Anderson: The four qualities technology CEOs need in the Age of Innovation in Hub Magazine (January 2017 issue. P.31).
