Transformational Development ProgramsWhat to Do When You’re at a Career Inflection Point

September 2024

2 mins read

Share

Share on LinkedIn

Share via Email

At the crossroads of change, every decision and direction can redefine our path. Whether you're contemplating a career shift, seeking personal growth, or facing a pivotal life moment, the journey ahead demands resources that resonate with your aspirations and challenges. When we work with leaders at varying levels within their organizations, we often ask them to take time to reflect on guiding questions:

What motivates you and does your leadership identity align with your core values and personal fulfillment?

How can you adapt your leadership style during this inflection point without losing your core values?

Can you think of a situation where you had to balance the demands of change with staying true to yourself as a leader? How can you do that again?

What are some strategies that can help you maintain both your professional identity and personal fulfillment during the transition?

How can support systems, such as mentors or networks, help you stay grounded while evolving as a leader?

As you begin to think about what may be next for you, we have curated a collection of resources, ranging from insightful podcasts and transformative books to thought-provoking articles and self-discovery exercises, designed to guide and inspire you.
