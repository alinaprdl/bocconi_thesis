Lose the Resume, Land the Job complements Korn Ferry's newest offering for professional-level job seekers, KF Advance.

Lose the Resume, Land the Job complements Korn Ferry's newest offering for professional-level job seekers, Korn Ferry Advance (www.KFAdvance.com). Korn Ferry Advance offers online assessments, resume review, career coaching, and other services that leverage Korn Ferry's vast IP and our more than 70 years of consulting expertise.

Through Korn Ferry Advance, job seekers find the expertise they need and the encouragement they desire to find the career path that will bring them more purpose and passion and elevate their performance.
