In this episode of the Remarkable Retail Podcast, Sally Elliott, co-leader of Spencer Stuart's Global Retail Practice, discusses the increase in leadership turnover within retail and why retailers should be investing early in their talent pipelines. She also spotlights the three “superpowers” of today’s retail CEOs, and the new skills and leadership style needed to succeed in an era of permanent transformation.
