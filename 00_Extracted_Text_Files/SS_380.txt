2021 CMO Summit

Highlights from our 19th Annual CMO Summit, where two leaders shared their experience on the path from CMO to CEO.

What does it take for a chief marketing officer to make the leap into the CEO role? And what career skills and experiences can prepare a CMO for the top job? At the 2021 CMO Summit, we were joined by two leaders who successfully made that transition from marketing leadership to the top corporate role: Michele Buck, board chair, president and CEO of The Hershey Company, and Mary Dillon, executive chair and former CEO of Ulta Beauty.
