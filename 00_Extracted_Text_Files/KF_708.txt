What Is Korn Ferry Sell, and How Does It Enhance Sales Effectiveness and Talent Success?

Korn Ferry Sell is an opportunity and account management solution, native to Salesforce & Microsoft Dynamics, that enables an organization to incorporate the Miller Heiman methodology into the day-to-day processes of the sellers. As sellers use the tools, Korn Ferry Sell captures methodology-based data directly into the CRM which can then be utilized to drive actions that will lead to repeatable success. Korn Ferry Sell also enables Sales Managers to provide real-time coaching and give their teams access to learning and development content on-demand.

What Types of Organizations Typically Benefit Most from Korn Ferry Sell?

Korn Ferry Sell is particularly beneficial for organizations across industries that recognize their growth aspirations are being impacted by external factors and that they are being held back by the current skill set of their commercial team.

What Are the Key Benefits of Using Korn Ferry Sell?

Korn Ferry Sell combines methodology and technology: a proven sales methodology (powered by Miller Heiman) combined with the Korn Ferry Sell platform, which integrates directly with both Salesforce and MS Dynamics. Sales teams can use the tool to:

Formulate strategic plans directly in the sales opportunity record backed by our proven sales methodology
Receive specific prompts on the best actions to take to close the deal
Get insight into what moves a deal along with an embedded Blue Sheet (Strategic Opportunity Plan)
See the opportunity health, progress, and deal pipeline without leaving Salesforce
Build Strategic Account Plans directly in the account record and make it accessible
Access real-time online training resources to drive sales and talent success
Access micro-learning, recommended actions, and full digital courses in our sales methodology
