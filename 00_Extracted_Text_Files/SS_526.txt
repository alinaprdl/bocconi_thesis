A core responsibility of the nominating/governance committee is the oversight of the composition of the board and its committees. Board and committee composition lie at the heart of board effectiveness.

The ideal board comprises a diverse group of directors from widely varying backgrounds offering complementary expertise who work well as a team and who possess the skills necessary for board and committee work. The ability to recruit the right directors and integrate them successfully is one of the clearest indicators of a high-functioning board.

This article highlights best practices and considerations for board composition and board assessment, including:

Recruitment considerations, including business strategy, independence requirements, committee needs and diversity

Onboarding best practices

Board leadership

Turnover trends

Corporate governance principles

Continuing director education

Board evaluations - five key principles

Individual director evaluations

Board evaluation disclosures
