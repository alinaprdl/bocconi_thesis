Russell Reynolds Conversation with the U.S. Chamber of Commerce on the FTC’s Ban on Non-Competes

Russell Reynolds Associates and the U.S Chamber of Commerce recently hosted a discussion on the FTC’s new ruling on non-competes. This event convened General Counsels, Chief Human Resources Officers, and Government Affairs leaders to explore the implications of the ruling, understand compliance, and discuss its impact across industries.
