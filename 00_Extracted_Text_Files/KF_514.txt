Unified, Integrated, Expanded: A Bold Step Forward for the Korn Ferry Talent Suite

In today’s fast-evolving talent landscape, agility is everything. That’s why we’re thrilled to provide our latest quarterly update to the Korn Ferry Talent Suite—enhancements that bring deeper integration, smarter unification, and greater scalability to how organizations attract, engage, and grow talent.

This release is more than an update—built with the future of work in mind, it empowers HR leaders, people managers, and employees with a more connected, intuitive experience that drives real impact across the employee lifecycle.

Let’s break down what’s new, and why it matters for building a workforce that’s ready for what’s next.

Unifying Our Vision for Talent

We’re taking tangible steps forward in delivering a fully unified Korn Ferry Talent Suite, connecting all our digital solutions and powerful IP into streamlined, intuitive workflows. This ongoing work is designed to help HR leaders and people managers make faster, smarter talent decisions—from hiring to succession planning—while providing employees a clearer understanding of their roles and development paths. The result is a cohesive ecosystem that boosts clarity, efficiency, and agility throughout the entire talent journey.

Groundbreaking HCM Integrations

Our new integrations with Workday and SAP SuccessFactors represent a major leap forward. By embedding Korn Ferry’s robust Success Profiles and proven IP directly into these leading HCM platforms, we're empowering HR leaders with deeper insights to make talent decisions confidently and swiftly. People managers experience reduced operational complexity, employees see a closer alignment between their skills and organizational needs, and the entire organization benefits from a more strategic approach to talent management at scale while supercharging their HCM investment.

Deep Integration to Drive Sales Excellence

We've meaningfully integrated Korn Ferry Sell with our assessment capabilities to deliver targeted insights and agile strategies specifically designed to enhance sales performance. Sales leaders and HR professionals gain precise visibility into skill gaps and tailored development recommendations, enabling people managers to elevate their teams more effectively. Employees benefit from personalized guidance, while organizations achieve improved sales outcomes and measurable growth. This integration is about powering sales teams to consistently perform at their highest levels.

Expanding Commitment to Fairness and Equity

The launch of Korn Ferry Pay Equity further demonstrates our deep commitment to helping organizations build fair, inclusive workplaces. Equipped with robust analytics and clear benchmarks, HR leaders can proactively address pay gaps. People managers gain transparency to confidently communicate compensation decisions, and employees experience enhanced fairness and trust. For organizations, it means fostering a culture that values integrity, equity, and inclusion.
