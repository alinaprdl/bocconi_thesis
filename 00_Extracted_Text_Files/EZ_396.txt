Egon Zehnder

Consumer Brainteaser 2018

Mobility in the Future, and its Impact on Consumer Behavior, Retail Dynamics and on Our Personal Lives

The Consumer Brainteaser addresses the CEOs of the leading Swiss-based consumer companies and the CMOs of the major global companies in Switzerland.

This will be a lively and inspirational gathering in a fun and relaxed atmosphere aiming to tease your brains through interactive discussions on the following topic: Mobility of the future and its impact on consumer & retail behavior, as well as on personal lives.

Egon Zehnder would be honored to welcome you

on Monday, July 9, 2018 at 18:00
at SBB Westlink, Vulkanplatz 11, 8048 Zürich-Altstetten

We request your understanding that this exclusive invitation is for you personally and is not transferable.

18.00 Aperitifs and exploring ways of future mobility @ SBB

19.00 Braintease-Introductions followed by interactive dinner conversations in a rotating set-up at the SBB Restaurant Westlink

21.30 Nightcap with "Pop-up" Bar

RSVP by telephone or e-mail to

Jennifer Trepp, Egon Zehnder
T +41 44 267 69 03
Jennifer.trepp@egonzehnder.com
